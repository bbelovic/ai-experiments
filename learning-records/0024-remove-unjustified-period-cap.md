# Removing an unjustified tool policy

The learner questioned the split between positive-count validation in the request record and the four-period tool maximum, then requested removal of the maximum after discussing its lack of a concrete requirement. The current contract accepts any positive int period count and retains the tool's null-request check; Codex made the requested code, test, and lesson updates. Future operational limits should follow an explicit resource or response-size requirement rather than preserving this exercise's arbitrary cap.
